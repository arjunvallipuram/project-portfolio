# About Me

I am a Mechanical Engineering graduate from the University of British Columbia with hands-on experience in mechanical design, CAD modelling, engineering documentation, and manufacturing. I enjoy tackling practical engineering challenges and developing solutions that combine sound mechanical design with real-world functionality.

Through my internship at WhiteWater West Industries and my academic projects, I have developed experience using SolidWorks, working with engineering drawings, managing technical documentation, and fabricating mechanical components. I am interested in opportunities where I can continue developing my design and manufacturing skills while contributing to the development of practical engineering solutions.
